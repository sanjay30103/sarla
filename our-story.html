Who we are

Home-style food, hotel-grade hospitality.

Sarla is the restaurant and bar of Hotel Sarla Inn on Khora Road, Sector 62 — a neighbourhood that never stops working. We wanted to give it a place to slow down: a warm room, a busy tandoor and food that tastes like someone cared.

Our kitchen cooks the dishes North India loves most — dal makhani simmered overnight, butter chicken finished in white butter, biryani sealed on dum — alongside street-style chaat, South Indian favourites and Indo-Chinese for the cravings that won't wait.

Whether you are a hotel guest, a team on a lunch break or a family out for Sunday dinner, you'll be welcomed the Indian way: Atithi Devo Bhava — the guest is god.

Our philosophy

Three things we never compromise on.

01

Fresh, every day

Vegetables bought every morning, paneer made fresh, breads rolled to order. If it is not fresh, it does not leave our kitchen.

02

Spices, ground in-house

We roast and grind our own garam masala, kitchen king and chaat masalas in small batches — that is where the flavour lives.

03

Hospitality first

Your water topped up before you ask, a kid-friendly tweak to a dish, a birthday dessert on the house. Small things, done every time.

Head Chef

Team Sarla

From the kitchen

“A dal that has cooked all night tastes different. Guests can't always say why — but they always come back.”

Our chefs bring years of experience from the kitchens of North India. Their rule is simple: cook every dish the long way. Marinades rest overnight, gravies are built from scratch and the tandoor is fired up before the first guest walks in.
