Sarla

From our kitchen to your table

Rooted in Tradition.
Served with Love.

Tucked inside Hotel Sarla Inn on Khora Road, Sarla is where Sector 62 comes to eat like it's home. Our recipes travel from the family kitchens of Punjab and Awadh — slow-cooked, generously spiced and finished with care.

From quick working lunches for the offices next door to long family dinners and evening drinks at the bar, every plate leaves our kitchen the way we would serve it at home.

“Good food is the warmest welcome.”

The Sarla Kitchen

Dum Cooking

Biryanis and curries sealed and slow-cooked on a low flame, so every grain carries the full depth of its spices.

Fresh Masalas

Whole spices roasted and ground in-house every morning — no packaged mixes, just honest flavour.
